# Future tool evaluation without interrupting the selected build

Bonsai is a candidate for the next constructed-building build, not a migration step for existing Tresendar work. It authors IFC inside Blender. Preserve the IFC architectural master, stable element IDs, source specification and separate Blender art/export derivatives; a downstream GLB or `.blend` alone is not the complete parametric IFC project. Keep geometry authority unambiguous and manual art changes away from regenerated structural data.

Before adoption, check installed Blender/Bonsai compatibility. Use two connected source-shaped rooms, an opening and an elevation change; change dimensions, reopen IFC, confirm element/semantic identity, transforms, evaluated geometry, UV/PBR export and native supported walking. Compare total revision and export effort with the existing numerical Blender method. IFC classes do not alone determine secret/door/collision behavior or load capacity. Terrain, organic caves and hero art need not pass through BIM. FreeCAD/CadQuery/build123d or commercial CAD are alternatives only when their reliability and handoff justify another authoring environment.

3D AI Studio is an optional future acquisition adapter. Its documented MCP includes alternative generators, low-poly conversion, UVs and material creation; advanced texturing/rigging/segmentation/Flow have website-only limitations. A paid connection and claims of game-ready output do not establish access, source fidelity or better results. Follow the common acquisition contract and benchmark specifically approved jobs before replacing a working provider. Do not add subscriptions or spend credits from a tool recommendation.

References checked 2026-10-26: https://docs.bonsaibim.org/studio/quickstart/create_model.html ; https://docs.bonsaibim.org/studio/guides/authoring/advanced_modeling/parametric_geometry.html ; https://docs.3daistudio.com/mcp/capabilities . Treat dated vendor capabilities as documentation, not measured engine acceptance.
