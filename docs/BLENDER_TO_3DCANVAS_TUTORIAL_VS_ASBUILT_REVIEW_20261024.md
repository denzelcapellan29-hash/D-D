## Exact contract issue detected (no edits performed)
`import_chunks_v02.py` `native_candidates` builds a candidate with `collision:True, sight:True, camera_collision:True` for every source chunk and, for `interactive_door`, applies door state/style at Tile level. This may both obscure sight from floors and animate the wrong pivot. Our exporter preserves mesh custom `extras`, creating an opportunity to fix these at **source metadata level** rather than custom vendor patches.

**Recommended source metadata classes** (DESIGN_CHOICE, not implemented):
- `floor/stair`: native collision support yes; sight usually no; camera collision per actual GameCamera.
- `opaque wall / closed roof`: collision and sight yes where appropriate; floor-aware visibility/Levels needed.
- `grass / small decoration`: collisions off unless physically obstructive; sight off.
- `door frame`: stationary visible solid bearing geometry, portal interior must remain clear.
- `hinged leaf`: exact door source ID, known swing direction, true hinge mesh pivot (and handle hierarchy) with **per-mesh native tags** where compatible.
- `secrets`: explicit DM-proof tagging/visibility, never auto-inferred from source art.
