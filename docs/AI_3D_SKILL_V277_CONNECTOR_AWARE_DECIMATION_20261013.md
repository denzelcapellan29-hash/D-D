Blender mesh cleanup documentation. Cave Meshy GLBs are often dense/irregular/open topology and may carry packed 2K PBR. Reduction must preserve visible rock silhouette, texture UVs, normal maps, material IDs, and important sharp and thin features; compare multiple ratios at identical camera and scene settings, record true evaluated triangles/render cost. Do not assume generic 20% ratio is always safe.

**Process policy:** SOURCE immutable GLB → define metric grid and common floor/wall/roof physical mating interface (frames/normals/tolerances/coverage) → independent Meshy-derived geometry/intake source with connector-protection masks → compare Collapse candidate ratios e.g. .70/.50/.30/.20 with actual UV/shading/camera tests; optional Planar on tested hidden planar connector regions; avoid blanket voxel remesh or indiscriminate `holes_fill` → verify full 2×2 joints (floor-floor, wall-wall, roof-roof, wall-floor, wall-roof) and traversal, bidirectional contact normal patches and visible art → select lowest triangle variant that PASSES all structural and PBR/visual gates → only then room replication. Maintain machine-readable `lod_manifest.json`, `connector_manifest.json`, `qa_manifest.json` with UNVERIFIED for missing data; numerical validator remains NOT IMPLEMENTED until coded and fixture regression tested. Art variants downstream of certified connector master. Stop after 2 comparable failed LODs or damaged mesh, return to method review.
