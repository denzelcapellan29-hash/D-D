# Architectural intent before connected-area expansion

Use this procedure for source-map interiors and when the DM rejects a build's architecture. It complements the integrated source/structure/art/world/native review. It grants no new geometry, asset, cost or runtime permissions.

## Establish one source blueprint

Read the actual map, room text and latest DM decisions. Freeze the exact map image/hash/crop and coordinate convention. Calibrate scale, origin and north from at least three non-collinear, distributed grid or architectural landmarks; record residuals, map distortion and uncertainty. If X/Y scale differs, justify rectification from the source instead of independently stretching rooms. Legacy transforms, approximate bounding boxes and connector locations are hypotheses until compared with the map. Never silently shift the source overlay separately per room to make a candidate appear correct.

Trace room and corridor envelopes, usable floor, wall faces/thickness, every normal/locked/secret/blocked opening, stair direction and landing, known elevations, chasm banks and bridges. Record portal owner and neighbor, source locator, center, clear width/height, opening/sweep and state. Distinguish explicit dimensions, measured raster estimates and design choices. A rectangular floor underneath walls does not alone define a room; compare its actual accessible envelope and wall/portal graph. Uncertain dimensions remain uncertain. Use the source to resolve routine choices; ask the DM only when a remaining ambiguity materially changes play or approved design.

## Compare the actual assembled architecture

Export or render an overhead plan from the evaluated saved geometry, with roofs hidden only for a labeled diagnostic. Overlay it on the single calibrated source blueprint. Inspect full connected circulation, not just object bounding boxes, collection membership or isolated rooms. List missing, extra, misplaced and wrongly sealed openings, room proportion/shape differences and stair/elevation contradictions. Include at least one deliberately wrong portal or room extent in the validation procedure and confirm that the source/layout review flags it. A connector gap negative control cannot substitute for this check.

Inspect the same revision at plausible approach, eye-height, turn, reverse and exit positions. Judge built-versus-natural form, wall returns and corners, jambs/lintels/thresholds, real wall/floor/ceiling bearings, roof ownership and opening clearance throughout the door sweep. A wall texture on a plane, a flat ceiling covering unresolved walls, a few columns placed in an incorrect cave envelope or a neat local adapter does not establish architectural intent. Real relief and coherent materials support architecture after the source shapes and circulation are right.

Check the same representative connection in the native runtime. Token center-point support or a short native route does not certify a complete body/camera envelope, the source floorplan or the whole building. Record precisely what each test measures and keep unresolved checks visible. Capture through a functioning renderer; sky-only, black, stale or minimized-app captures are not visual evidence.

## Repair the causal error and honor rejection

When source or architectural review fails, hold affected expansion, broad repetition, further decoration intended to hide it and release. Preserve originals and still-valid evidence, but do not preserve an erroneous blueprint simply because old sockets or tests match it. Correct wrong room shapes, openings or levels in versioned derivatives, update dependent neighbors and retest the affected connections. Do not force the official route to bend around inherited mistakes. No wholesale restart or unrelated generic pilot is required: repair one existing connected slice.

A direct DM architectural rejection immediately sets the affected candidate's architecture/art acceptance to FAIL. “Continue” does not waive it. Prepare a concrete corrected slice, source overlay and matched player-eye views for review before expanding the affected family. Only the DM can grant visual acceptance; a procedural update grants none.

## Review record and reproducibility

Keep one compact ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW in project evidence: source blueprint/hash/calibration and uncertainties; exact saved source/assembly/runtime versions and membership; source-overlay and matched views; portal/route/elevation comparison; construction, bearing and clearance findings; PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED per check; DM decision; preserved work; next one causal repair. Keep private source pixels, coordinates, secrets and live IDs out of this reusable skill.

At a visual milestone, show the selected review assembly in the app or explicitly identify a stale visible file. Reconcile Blender and native inventories; do not present an old open file or a newer unmatched import as the current common result. Back up unsaved work before switching views. Parameterize geometry from the verified blueprint so replay reproduces the same layout, rather than accumulating socket-fitting scripts and contradictory checkpoints.
