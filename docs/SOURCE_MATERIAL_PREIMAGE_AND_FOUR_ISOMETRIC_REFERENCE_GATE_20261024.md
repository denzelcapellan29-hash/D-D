# Pre-Image Source Material Gate and Four-Isometric Reference Workflow
**Decision and installed procedure:** 2026-10-24, AI 3D Asset Workflow **v2.7.64** (private plugin). Public document is a pointer and nonsecret contract, not a reproduction of the private adventure.

**Prior status:** G0 and AR0 said read official source before design, but did not require per-material evidence and explicit handling of source silence. v2.7.64 adds binding `skills/meshy-asset-pipeline/references/SOURCE_MATERIAL_AND_FOUR_ISO_REFERENCE_GATE_V2764.md`, linked from Art/Skill and route procedures.

**Before generating any image:** read actual pertinent official text, map, illustrations/captions and latest DM decisions, not chat recall or an unrelated art asset. For each appearance attribute record `SOURCE_EXPLICIT`, `SOURCE_VISUAL`, `DM_APPROVED`, `SOURCE_CONTEXT`, `SOURCE_SILENT`, `SOURCE_CONFLICT` or `UNVERIFIED`, plus private page/location and material choice. Published explicit material controls; map diagram alone does not specify color; context suggests possibilities, not official facts; silence requires actually checking the material, not just assuming absence. When silent, choose a period- and location-coherent `DESIGN_CHOICE`; conflicting/unread source blocks source-certified asset generation and paid Meshy task until resolved. Never invent new portal/topology/secret just because visuals are unspecified.

**Validated native image format:** a **single 2×2 reference image containing exactly four elevated three-quarter isometric views of ONE unchanged asset**, no straight-overhead, no fifth, labels or accessory panels. In this chat, user accepted wall and floor four-view reference compositions on 2026-10-24, but they are not source-certified and do not prove 3D identical vertices. Audit same-object identity. Only a valid deliberate 2×2 turnaround may be split into four ordered individual frames for `meshy_multi_image_to_3d`; a random collage may NOT be cropped into a fabricated multiview set. Meshy texture text controls material; CAD independently owns physical sockets, contact, scale, doors and movement.
